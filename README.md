## 🐈 About Me

<div align="center">
  <img src="assets/me.jpg" width="140" style="border-radius:50%">

I am a backend-focused software developer with a strong interest in building reliable, well-structured systems. My work is mainly centered around core application logic, data persistence, and system behavior rather than user interfaces. I enjoy designing solutions where correctness, clarity, and maintainability are the primary goals.

Most of my projects are console-based or backend-oriented, as they allow me to focus on problem-solving, architecture, and technical fundamentals such as concurrency, networking, and database interaction. I prefer working close to the data layer, modeling information carefully and ensuring that applications behave predictably under different conditions.

Currently, I am transitioning toward building REST APIs using Spring Boot, expanding my experience from standalone applications to service-oriented architectures. I am particularly interested in backend design decisions, trade-offs, and best practices that lead to scalable and maintainable systems over time.

Outside of coding, I value a calm and focused working environment, often accompanied by my cat quietly supervising from nearby.
